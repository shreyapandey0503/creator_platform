FAQ

Good questions.

Where does the creator data come from?

From you — your existing sheets and agency lists. We clean and structure them. YouTube channels and posts can be enriched from the official YouTube Data API; Instagram stats come from your records or creator-authorised connections (licensed data providers can be added).

Is my data private?

Yes. Every company gets its own separate creator database. Your rate cards, notes and campaign results are never visible to any other client.

When am I billed?

When a campaign launches we invoice the creators already confirmed; creators confirmed later are added to a follow-up invoice. You never pay for creators who were only shortlisted.

Can my team use it?

Yes — add teammates from Settings. There's no per-seat charge.

What file format do I upload?

Any CSV with creator names and profile links or handles. Columns like followers, city, age, category, languages and rate per reel are detected automatically. Download our sample from the upload page to see an example.
